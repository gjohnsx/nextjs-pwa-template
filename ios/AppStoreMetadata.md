- Description: Yen Sense is a fast, private pocket converter for Japan travel. Enter a yen amount, see the USD estimate instantly, add up shopping totals, refresh the live rate when you have a connection, and keep a manual or cached rate for offline use.

  The built-in Practice mode helps you learn common Japan travel prices before you are standing at a vending machine, train gate, cafe, or shop.
