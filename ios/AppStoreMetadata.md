## Version 1.0

- Subtitle: Pocket yen converter for Japan
- Promotional text: Convert yen to dollars fast, save your rate offline, and practice common Japan travel prices.
- Description: Yen Sense is a fast pocket converter for Japan travel. Enter a yen amount, see the USD estimate instantly, refresh the live JPY to USD rate when you have a connection, and keep a manual or cached rate for offline use. The built-in practice mode helps you learn common travel prices before you are standing at a vending machine, train gate, cafe, or shop.
