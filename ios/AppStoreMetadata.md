## Version 1.0

- Subtitle: Japan Currency Converter
- Promotional text: Practice real Japan prices, add up shopping totals, and convert yen fast with no ads, no tracking, and offline rates.
- Description: Yen Sense is a fast, private pocket converter for Japan travel. Enter a yen amount, see the USD estimate instantly, add up shopping totals, refresh the live rate when you have a connection, and keep a manual or cached rate for offline use.

  The built-in Practice mode helps you learn common Japan travel prices before you are standing at a vending machine, train gate, cafe, or shop.

  Yen Sense Pro is a one-time unlock for travelers who want more depth: the full Practice deck with history, extra home currencies, and the Home/Lock Screen rate widget. The free core converter stays useful forever, with no ads, no tracking, and no subscription.
